The founder

Natasha Sizlo

Natasha Sizlo is the founder of mouurn and the author of All Signs Point to Paris: A Memoir of Love, Loss, and Destiny (Mariner Books, 2022) — a Los Angeles Times bestseller. Her writing has appeared in Variety, Cosmopolitan, Detour, and Shape.

She built mouurn out of her own experience of loss — losing her father, and later her home in the Los Angeles fires — and the discovery that even the people who love us most rarely know how to show up for grief. mouurn is the guide she needed, and didn't have.

She lives in Los Angeles with her two children.

"To show up for someone in mourning is one of the most profound acts of love."
The memoir
All Signs Point to Paris
A Memoir of Love, Loss, and Destiny · Mariner Books · 2022 · LA Times Bestseller · A People "Best New Books" Pick

The compulsively readable true story of one woman's search for a second chance at love — a story about grief and sisterhood, about following your stars and taking charge of your destiny, and about being open to a little magic.

Divorced, heartbroken, and reeling from her father's death, Natasha sets off for Paris with her sister and two best friends in search of the man the stars say she was destined for. A memoir that reads like a novel.
